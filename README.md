# HR Analytics Suite: Intelligent Recruitment & Workforce Management

A comprehensive data science ecosystem for Human Resources, covering the entire employee lifecycle—from initial recruitment screening to long-term retention and compensation benchmarking.

##  Project Components

### 1. Recruitment & Hiring Predictor (The Digital Auditor)
Automates the initial screening process by analyzing candidate scores to predict hiring viability.
* **Business Impact:** Reduces manual screening time by 80% (~200 hours saved).
* **Files:** `4 (1).ipynb`, `4 (1).csv`, `4 (1).png`, `4 (2).png`

### 2. Employee Attrition & Retention Engine
Identifies high-risk employees and key drivers of turnover using Random Forest classification.
* **Performance:** 93.09% ROC-AUC | 85.59% F1-Score.
* **Files:** `2.ipynb`, `2.csv`, `2.png` through `2 (5).png`

### 3. Salary & Compensation Benchmarking
Regression analysis to ensure competitive pay scales based on experience and age.
* **Files:** `3.ipynb`, `3.csv`, `3.png`

##  Key Insights Across Modules
- **Efficiency:** The suite saves approximately $4,900–$5,000 in operational costs per recruitment cycle.
- **Fairness:** Includes a DEI (Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion) audit to ensure AI-driven decisions are unbiased.
- **Drivers:** Skill scores and interview performance are the highest predictors for hiring, while overtime and income drive attrition.

##  Setup
1. Clone the repo.
2. Install requirements: `pip install pandas scikit-learn matplotlib seaborn joblib`.
3. Run the `.ipynb` files in sequence to see the full lifecycle analysis.
